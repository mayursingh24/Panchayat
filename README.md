PanchAyat — Super Society Management Platform

Developed during a hackathon, PanchAyat is a digital-first platform built to simplify everyday community operations and bring structure to modern residential living. It connects residents and administrators on a unified system, enabling seamless communication, faster issue resolution, and efficient society management.

From raising and tracking complaints to managing maintenance payments, accessing notices, participating in events, and interacting with neighbors — PanchAyat transforms traditional processes into a smart, organized, and transparent digital ecosystem. The platform reduces offline dependency and ensures all essential services are accessible in a few clicks.

---

🚀 What Makes PanchAyat Powerful?

- 📝 Complaint Management – Raise, track, and resolve issues with real-time updates
- 📢 Smart Notices – Instant communication for announcements
- 💳 Digital Payments – Simplified maintenance tracking and transactions
- 💬 Community Chat – Direct communication between residents and admins
- 🤖 AI Assistant – Smart support for queries and complaints
- 📅 Event Management – Organize and engage in community activities

---
